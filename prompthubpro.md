# Privacy Policy

**App name:** PromptHub Pro (Android package `com.prompthubpro.prompt_hub_pro`)
**Developer:** CodeOrbit Labs
**Contact:** er.varunvision@gmail.com
**Last updated:** September 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the **PromptHub Pro** mobile application (referred to below as "the app", "we", "our", or "us"), published on Google Play by **CodeOrbit Labs**, handles your information.

A large AI prompt library with a guided Prompt Builder and a Prompt Enhancer for creators.

The short version, in plain English: **the app has no accounts, no advertising and no third-party tracking. The only thing that leaves your device is a random identifier the app invents for itself, so that we can count devices and stop abuse.**

The rest of this document explains exactly what that means.

---

## 1. What the app sends to us

Every request the app makes to our server at `mobileappprompt.emojireach.com` carries these three things, and nothing else:

| What | Why we need it | Is it personal? |
|---|---|---|
| A random app identifier | Rate limiting, and counting roughly how many devices use the app | Pseudonymous. The app generates this value itself on first launch. It is **not** your advertising ID, **not** your Android ID, and **not** derived from your hardware, Google account, phone number or email. |
| App name and version | To serve the right content and keep older versions working | No |
| Platform (`android` or `ios`) | To serve the right content format | No |

Under Google Play's Data safety definitions, the random app identifier counts as **Device or other IDs**, and it is **collected** — because it leaves your device. It is **not shared**, because it only ever reaches our own server.

---

## 2. What we do NOT collect

We do **not** collect, store, transmit or have access to:

- Your name, email address, phone number or postal address
- Any account or login — the app does not have one
- Your location, precise or approximate
- Your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or files
- Your advertising identifier — there is no advertising SDK in the app
- Crash logs or analytics events from any third-party SDK — there are none
- Any payment or financial information

There is no sign-up, no profile and no password to lose.

---

## 3. What stays on your device

The app remembers a few things locally so it works between sessions. None of it is sent to us:

| What | Where | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Your saved and favourited prompts | Local app storage | So your starred prompts are there next time |
| Your recent searches | Local app storage | So you can repeat a search quickly |
| Theme, text size and layout preferences | Local app storage | So the app looks the way you set it |
| Whether you have seen the intro screens | Local app storage | So you only see them once |
| The random app identifier described above | Local app storage | So the same device is not counted twice |

Uninstalling the app deletes all of it, the identifier included. Reinstalling generates a brand-new identifier with no link to the old one.

---

## 4. Who we share your data with

**Nobody.** The three items in section 1 go only to our own server. We do not sell, rent, trade or share them with advertisers, data brokers, analytics providers or any other third party.

The single exception is a legal one: if required by law, a court order or a valid government request, we would disclose what we hold — which is a random identifier and an app version.

---

## 5. The content shown in the app

PromptHub Pro displays a catalogue of text prompts and example images for use with AI image and video generation tools. a large AI prompt library with a guided Prompt Builder and a Prompt Enhancer for creators.

That catalogue is curated and reviewed by us before publication and served from our own systems. **Users cannot upload, post or publish content into the app**, so nothing you do inside the app is visible to anyone else.

Some example images in the catalogue are AI-generated. Prompts are provided for you to copy into whichever AI tool you choose — what happens in that third-party tool is governed by that tool's own privacy policy, not this one, and we have no visibility into it.

---

## 6. How we protect your data

- Every connection between the app and our servers uses **HTTPS**. The app does not permit unencrypted connections.
- Each request is **cryptographically signed**, so our server can reject traffic that did not come from a genuine copy of the app.
- Access to our servers is limited to authorised administrators.

---

## 7. How long we keep it

The random identifier and its request counts are kept for up to **12 months**, then deleted. We keep them only to detect abuse and to understand roughly how many devices use the app.

---

## 8. Deleting your data

There are two ways:

- **Uninstall the app.** This immediately and permanently removes the identifier and everything stored on your device. Afterwards nothing links you to the records on our side.
- **Ask us.** Email [er.varunvision@gmail.com](mailto:er.varunvision@gmail.com) and we will delete the server-side records for your app identifier. We will need that identifier, which you can find in the app under Settings.

We aim to respond to any request within 30 days.

---

## 9. Children

PromptHub Pro is not directed at children and is rated for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has used the app and you would like their data removed, contact us and we will act on it.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, and to data portability. Because we hold only a random identifier with no way to connect it to you as a person, the practical route to all of these is the deletion process in section 8. Email [er.varunvision@gmail.com](mailto:er.varunvision@gmail.com) and we will help.
